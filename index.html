Our Services

Guest Communication

We respond quickly to enquiries and oversee all guest comms before, during and after their stay.
Our professional and effective manner ensures the optimal booking experience for you and your guest.

Calendar Management

We manage all bookings and maintain calendar updates to promptly reflect date changes and cancellations - allowing you to have maximise occupancy and effective date searches that lead to bookings.

Cleaning Coordination

We schedule in cleaners after each stay, share changeover checklists and quality control all cleanings. We also manage the deep-cleaning programme - ensuring a consistent high standard of cleanliness.

Pricing Adjustments

Light price adjustments for seasons, demand, or local events allow you to optimise booking rates and revenue. We monitor general occupancy trends, applying our market knowledge and strategy.

Service Tiers and Pricing Structure

To help you get the most from your short-stay property, Raglan Hostworks provides three tiered management options. Each tier reflects the level of care, optimisation, and day-to-day involvement needed to keep your listing performing at its best, while giving you control over how much support you’d like.

Basic Management

This tier includes all the essential day-to-day operations required to run your Airbnb listing smoothly. It’s ideal if you’re looking for reliable, professional management without the extra marketing layers.

We charge 12-14% of the net revenue for the Basic Management Package


Included in Basic Management:

Full guest communication (inquiries, booking questions, stay support)

Calendar management and booking approvals

Listing optimisation and updates

Review and reputation management

Cleaning and turnover coordination

Basic pricing adjustments

Monthly performance summaries


Best for: Owners wanting solid management while remaining involved in strategic decisions or handling some elements themselves.

Full-Service Management

This tier is designed to be a comprehensive, hands-off solution. It includes everything in the Basic tier, plus deeper operational support and proactive improvement of the property’s performance.

We charge 16-18% of the net revenue for the Full-Service Management Package


Included in Full-Service Management:

Dynamic pricing management for maximum revenue

Inventory and supplies management

Maintenance coordination (repairs, inspections, contractor liaison)

Compliance guidance (local requirements, safety checks, insurance advice)

Detailed monthly performance reporting with recommendations

Seasonal listing optimisation to increase occupancy


Best for: Owners wanting full professional oversight and optimisation of their property without having to be involved in daily operations.

Premium Management + Marketing
